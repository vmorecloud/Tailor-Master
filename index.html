Depending on how you use the App, Tailor Master may collect and store the following information:

The App may allow you to enter information about your tailoring customers, including:

  • Customer name

You should only enter customer information when you have a legitimate business reason to do so and are authorized to provide or manage that information.

The App may store clothing measurement information, which may include:

  • Shirt length
  • Trouser length
  • Waist size
  • Other clothing measurements entered by the user

These measurements are used to help manage tailoring orders and customer requirements.

1.3 Order and Business Information

The App may collect information related to your tailoring business, including:

  • Order numbers
  • Order dates
  • Order status
  • Delivery dates
  • Order amounts
  • Payment status
  • Amounts paid or pending

If account registration or login functionality is introduced, we may collect information required to create and authenticate your account, such as:

The App may automatically process limited technical information necessary for the App to operate properly, such as:

We do not use this information to identify you personally unless required for providing or securing the service.
